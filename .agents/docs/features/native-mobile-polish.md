# Native mobile polish

## Goal

After basic mobile builds are reliable, make Onyx comfortable on touch devices and integrate with platform task-capture workflows.

## Candidate scope

- Touch targets, pull-to-refresh, delete gestures, and haptics
- Background sync and due-date notifications
- Share targets/extensions and app shortcuts
- Widgets and platform-specific storage integrations
- Store/TestFlight/F-Droid distribution readiness

Prioritize shared, high-frequency interactions before platform-specific integrations.
