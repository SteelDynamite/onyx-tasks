# Startup performance is unmeasured

## Debt

The desktop GUI has a sub-300ms cold-start aspiration without a repeatable benchmark or measurements.

## Impact

Performance claims cannot guide prioritization or detect regressions.

## Why we took it

Functional GUI and cross-platform work had higher priority during pre-alpha development.

## Retirement condition

Define representative hardware and cold-start boundaries, automate or document measurement, record a baseline, and either meet the target or replace it with evidence-based budgets.
